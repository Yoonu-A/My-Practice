Kumar Sangakaara

Kumar Sangakkara is a former Sri Lankan cricketer
and captain of the Sri Lankan national team.
He is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen
in the history of cricket. Sangakkara was known for his elegant
batting style, exceptional wicket-keeping skills,
and leadership qualities. He has numerous records to his name,
including being one of the highest run-scorers in international cricket.
After retiring from professional cricket, he has also been involved
in cricket administration and commentary.
