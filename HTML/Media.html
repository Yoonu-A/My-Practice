Media Tag

Audio Tag

The audio tag is used to embed sound content in documents. It may contain one or more audio sources, represented using the src attribute or the source element: the browser will choose the most suitable one. It can also be the child of a video tag.

Video Tag

The video tag is used to embed video content in a document. It may contain one or more video sources, represented using the src attribute or the source element: the browser will choose the most suitable one. It can also be the child of a video tag.
